Privacy and analytics

This course site is published by the owner of the tinix84 GitHub account. For privacy requests, use the contact details on that profile. Updated 22 October 2026.

Your choice

Reading these pages does not require analytics. Until you select “Accept analytics”, this site does not load Google Analytics or send analytics requests. “Reject analytics” leaves collection off. The “Analytics settings” button on every page lets you change your choice. Withdrawal stops future collection and removes this site's analytics cookies; it does not erase records already sent to Google.

What acceptance enables

Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-H0BY8YR2Q8) measures page visits, repeat browser visits and engagement. It can report device and browser information, approximate geographic information, referrer and interaction data. These are browser-based statistics, not proof of a person's identity. Google receives network information, including your IP address, when your browser contacts its services. Do not enter personal information into URLs.

The site disables Google signals and advertising personalization and sends page addresses without query strings or fragments. Google Analytics settings managed in the property can also affect collection and retention. No names, email addresses or student identifiers are intentionally sent by this site's code.

Storage and retention

The choice is stored in this browser's local storage under power-electronics-analytics-v1 for up to 180 days. Analytics cookies use the prefix pe_course, are scoped to this course's path, and are configured to expire after 180 days without extending their expiry on each visit. They distinguish browsers and sessions after consent. Browser controls can block or remove this storage.

Google processes analytics data under the site owner's Analytics account. Data may be processed outside your country. Google's contractual terms, transfer safeguards and the property administrator's retention settings apply. See Google's privacy policy and Google Analytics privacy information.

Hosting

GitHub Pages serves this site and may process connection and server-log data independently of analytics consent. See the GitHub privacy statement.
