Parallel Connection with Interphase Transformer

Parallel groups and interphase coupling

Two three-pulse groups feed the ends of an ideal center-tapped interphase winding.

At one instant their voltages are 110 and 90 V. The midpoint delivers 100 V while the winding absorbs the 20 V difference. Take the 20 V difference as constant for 100 µs. Define circulating current as half the difference between branch currents. The 2 mH inductance measured end to end then gives a circulating-current change of 20 V × 100 µs / 2 mH = 1 A. With 10 A initially in each branch the currents become 11 and 9 A. Total load current remains 20 A.

Practice exercise

The two ends of an ideal center-tapped interphase winding are at 110 and 90 V. The load draws 12 A shared equally. Find tap voltage, half-winding voltage magnitudes and branch currents.

Check your reasoning

The tap is at the mean, 100 V. Each half supports 10 V in magnitude. Each rectifier branch supplies 6 A.
