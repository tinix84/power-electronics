C. PV: specify the current direction before naming a resistance

At 18 V, delivered current is 4.323327 A. Its derivative is −0.451118 A/V. Thus the delivered-current incremental ratio is −2.216715 Ω.

Passive current is the negative of delivered current. Its derivative is +0.451118 A/V and its incremental resistance is +2.216715 Ω. The physical curve is unchanged by reversing the current reference.

For +0.2 V, the tangent predicts a −90.2236 mA delivered-current change. The nonlinear model gives −96.5150 mA. The change-error is about 6.52%.

The model's maximum power occurs at approximately 17.214268 V and 4.599240 A. Delivered power is about 79.172553 W. At that point:

The passive incremental resistance is positive with the same magnitude. The MPP condition is a local power derivative, not a universal resistance for every panel operating point.

Use the nonlinear curve if a 6.52% current-change error exceeds the application's allowance. A smaller perturbation can justify a tangent after another error check. A light change adds a photocurrent perturbation, while temperature changes the diode parameters.
