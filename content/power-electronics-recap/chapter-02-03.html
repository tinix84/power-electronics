2.3 A useful first estimate: 1 µH/m for a two-wire loop

For an air-insulated pair of long, round, parallel conductors, define as the wire diameter and as the centre-to-centre spacing. The external loop inductance per unit length in the quasi-TEM model is:

For :

External loop inductance
2 0.53 µH/m
5 0.92 µH/m
10 1.20 µH/m
20 1.48 µH/m

Engineering rule for this recap: use 1 µH/m = 1 nH/mm as the first estimate for an ordinary two-wire loop of this order of spacing. The logarithmic geometry dependence explains why it is often useful. Air gives approximately vacuum permeability; it does not remove the geometry factor.

The expression excludes internal conductor inductance; a pair of nonmagnetic round wires with uniform low-frequency current adds approximately 0.1 µH/m internally, which diminishes as skin effect develops. MIT's electromagnetics notes provide the field and transmission-line background.
