12. Bode, LC filtering and Class-D amplifiers

The voltage- or current-transfer magnitude in decibels is:

A power ratio uses 10 log₁₀ instead. The two agree when the power ratio is the square of the amplitude ratio, as for voltages across equal resistances. In a factorized transfer function, each first-order pole contributes −20 dB/decade above its corner; each first-order zero contributes +20 dB/decade.

For ordinary left-half-plane factors, their phase contributions tend toward −90° and +90°, respectively. Add gain contributions in dB and add the phases; check the transition regions with the exact response.
