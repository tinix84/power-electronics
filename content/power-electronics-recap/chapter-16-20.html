16.20 Can two converters with triangular ripple produce an almost flat total current?

Predict. Two buck phases feed one output capacitor and a constant 10 A load. Each phase carries 5 A average. Use 12 V input, 6 V output, 100 kHz per phase and 10 µH per inductor, with continuous conduction. Does adding a second phase necessarily increase output-capacitor ripple current?

At ideal duty ratio 0.5, each inductor sees +6 V for 5 µs and −6 V for 5 µs. The peak-to-peak ripple is:

Each current swings between 3.5 A and 6.5 A. With simultaneous switching, their ripples add to 6 A peak-to-peak. With a 180° phase shift, one matched current rises exactly while the other falls; the sum is 10 A constant in this ideal steady-state case.

KCL gives the capacitor current, not the sum of individual RMS magnitudes:

For the perfectly matched interleaved case at this duty ratio, iC is zero. With in-phase switching it is a zero-mean triangle of 6 A peak-to-peak. Integrating it through an ideal 100 µF capacitor gives 75 mV peak-to-peak voltage ripple. This also follows from ΔVpp = ΔIpp/(8fsC) for this triangular capacitor current.

Break the cancellation deliberately. Keep the phase shift at 180° but change the second inductor to 12 µH, maintaining the stipulated 5 A mean phase currents. Its ripple is now 2.5 A peak-to-peak. The sum has 0.5 A peak-to-peak residual ripple, giving 6.25 mV peak-to-peak on the same ideal capacitor in the prescribed-current model.

Current timing / inductance Summed current ripple Ideal 100 µF voltage ripple
In phase, matched 10 µH 6 A peak-to-peak 75 mV peak-to-peak
180°, matched 10 µH 0 in this ideal case 0 steady-state ripple in this ideal case
180°, 10 µH and 12 µH 0.5 A peak-to-peak 6.25 mV peak-to-peak

Change one thing: shift phase, vary inductance, or change duty away from 50%. Cancellation depends on phase count and duty ratio; two phases do not eliminate ripple at every operating point. Timing errors, current-sharing errors, ESR/ESL, load transients and layout leave additional requirements. An output capacitor is still necessary.

Individual phase RMS currents and conduction losses also remain nonzero even when their summed AC component cancels. TI's multiphase buck guide discusses cancellation and practical limits.

Phase choice. Since two phases are already present, use 180° interleaving at this matched 50% operating point. It cancels their summed ripple without adding another phase. Simultaneous switching instead gives 75 mV peak-to-peak ripple through the stated ideal capacitor.

A single-phase design remains a simpler alternative if its current stress and filter meet the requirements. Keep the output capacitor and check the full duty range; this cancellation does not cover transients or mismatch.
