Diode-voltage change Exact current change Tangent prediction Error relative to exact change

A 10 mV perturbation looks small beside 0.650 V. It is not small beside the 26 mV exponential scale. Judge smallness against curvature and the required error.

The surrounding circuit sets the perturbation across the diode. A source variation can divide between a series resistor and . It need not appear entirely across the junction.

At a 2 mA bias, the same model gives approximately 13 Ω. A 1 kΩ series resistor then receives most of a small source-voltage change. Section 19.7 asks you to calculate that division.

At higher frequencies, junction and diffusion capacitances also matter. A constant resistor describes only the stated low-frequency local model. MIT's diode recitation develops the conductance and charge-storage terms.
