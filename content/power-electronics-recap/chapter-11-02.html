11.2 Series resonator: an impedance minimum

For a series R–L–C network:

At resonance the reactances cancel, so the input impedance is . Below resonance the network is capacitive; above resonance it is inductive. Individual L and C voltages may be much larger than the input because the reactive voltages cancel at the port.
