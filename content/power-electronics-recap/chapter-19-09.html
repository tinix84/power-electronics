19.9 An LED warms up: which quantity should the driver regulate?

A white light-emitting diode (LED) draws 350 mA at 25 °C. Its supply has a fixed 5 V rail. Can the current stay within ±5% as junction temperature rises to 125 °C?

Predict the current change before looking at the plots. Keep the LED junction temperature separate from ambient temperature and driver-component temperatures.

This is an original worked teaching case based on the Cree XLamp XP-E2 white family. The exercise and its solution have separate reading pages. The source data are typical values, not a worst-case model of every manufactured LED.
