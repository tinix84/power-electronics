12.2 Two poles: loaded LC

Take an ideal series L, followed by C in parallel with a resistive load R. Ignore inductor resistance for this model:

Far above its poles, this low-pass falls at −40 dB/decade and its phase approaches −180°. The natural frequency is not automatically a −3 dB corner for every Q. For the Butterworth choice Q = 1/√2, it is the −3 dB corner. Critical damping instead means Q = 0.5; these are different responses.
