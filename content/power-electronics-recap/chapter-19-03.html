The plotted curves include an illustrative triode-region extension. The derivative values above use the stated saturation equation. Keep every perturbed point in its intended operating region.

This square-law model illustrates derivatives. It is not a prediction for a selected power MOSFET or a nanoscale transistor. Short-channel effects, body variation, capacitances and self-heating can require a different model. The local-derivative method still applies where that model is differentiable.

Changing body voltage adds another controlled-current term. High-frequency models also require capacitances. MIT's MOSFET circuit-model notes develop these extensions.
