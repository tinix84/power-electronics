12.1 One pole: RC

For the unloaded RC low-pass:

At the corner, magnitude is 1/√2, or −3.010 dB, and phase is −45°. Well above the corner, the magnitude falls at −20 dB/decade, and phase approaches −90°. A decade means a frequency ratio of ten; an octave means a factor of two.
